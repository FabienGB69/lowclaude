Apply evolutionary / natural-selection thinking to the problem at hand.

When invoked with $ARGUMENTS (a problem, idea, or codebase area):

1. **Variation** — Generate 3-5 distinct approaches or solutions (mutations), each with a different strategy or trade-off.
2. **Selection pressure** — Evaluate each variant against the real constraints: performance, maintainability, time-to-ship, team skill, cost.
3. **Fitness score** — Rate each variant 1-10 on the key criteria.
4. **Survive & reproduce** — Take the fittest elements from the top variants and combine them into a recommended hybrid solution.
5. **Next generation** — Propose one iteration or refinement that could make the winner even stronger.

Think adversarially: what environment (scale, edge cases, team changes) would kill each solution?
